# Code of Conduct

LoongForge follows the [Linux Foundation Code of Conduct](https://lfprojects.org/policies/code-of-conduct/).
We want participation in issues, pull requests, documentation, discussions, and community channels to be respectful, inclusive, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Keep discussions technical, reproducible, and on topic.
- Respect maintainers' review workload and follow the repository's [contribution process](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Report behavior that makes the community unwelcoming or unsafe.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to [loongforge@baidu.com](mailto:loongforge@baidu.com).
Reports are handled confidentially by the LoongForge maintainers where possible.
Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a violation of this policy.

For the complete policy, enforcement expectations, and reporting guidance, see the
[Linux Foundation Code of Conduct](https://lfprojects.org/policies/code-of-conduct/).
