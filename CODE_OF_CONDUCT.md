# Code of Conduct

LoongForge bases its community standards on the [Linux Foundation Code of Conduct](https://lfprojects.org/policies/code-of-conduct/).
These standards apply to participation in project issues, pull requests,
documentation, discussions, and community channels.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Treat people fairly. Harassment, bullying, and discrimination are unacceptable.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Keep discussions technical, reproducible, and on topic.
- Respect maintainers' review workload and follow the repository's
  [contribution process](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Report behavior that makes the community unwelcoming or unsafe.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to [loongforge@baidu.com](mailto:loongforge@baidu.com).
LoongForge maintainers handle reports confidentially where possible.
Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith violates this
policy.
