About LoongForge

A training framework born from real-world, large-scale production workloads — and shared back with the community

🐉 Our Story

LoongForge is an open-source training framework developed by the Baidu AI Cloud Baige team, built to deliver faster training for mainstream LLMs, VLMs, diffusion, and embodied models — and thereby significantly reduce cost

LoongForge was open-sourced from AIAK-Training-LLM, a training acceleration suite delivered to enterprise customers across Education, Computer Vision, and Embodied AI on Baidu AI Cloud. After years of hardening under real production workloads, its largest runs reach 5,000+ XPUs

It now joins the Baige Loong open-source series — named after the traditional Chinese loong boat (龙舟), a symbol of coordinated power and forward momentum
